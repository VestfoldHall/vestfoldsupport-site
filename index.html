About this domain

vestfoldsupport.com is the address Vestfold Hall uses to carry support email on behalf of the shops that use it.

If you received a message from an address ending in @vestfoldsupport.com, it came from the shop you contacted. Each shop gets its own address here so your reply gets back to the right people. Shop owners can also send from their own domain instead.

We only send a reply to someone who wrote to a shop first, or a notification to the shop owner. No marketing goes out from this domain, and we don't sell or share addresses. If a message looked wrong, tell us at support@vestfoldhall.com and a person will read it.
